The Cornell method divides each page into three areas, so your notes are organized for review from the start.

## Step 1: Divide the page

Draw a narrow cue column on the left, a wide notes column on the right, and leave a strip at the bottom for a summary.

> **Quick tip:** Print a ready-made page first, so you don't spend class time drawing lines.

## Step 2: Take notes in the main column

Write key ideas in your own words. Short phrases work better than full sentences.

## Step 3: Add cues afterwards

After class, write questions or keywords in the left column that each note answers.
