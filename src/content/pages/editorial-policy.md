## Our standards

Articles aim to be clear, accurate and useful. We write in plain language, say what we do not know, and do not make promises about results.

## How we research

- Claims are checked against reputable sources, such as research papers, official bodies and recognised experts.
- Sources are listed at the end of each article.
- Every article shows its publication date and its last update.

## Health and wellbeing content

> **General information only.** Our health articles are not medical advice and cannot replace a qualified professional. If you are worried about your health, speak to a doctor or another qualified professional.
